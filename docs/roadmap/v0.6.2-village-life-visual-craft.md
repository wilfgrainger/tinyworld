# v0.6.2 Village Life & Visual Craft

**Status:** active release after merged v0.6.1 Visual Rescue.  
**Profile schema:** 11.

v0.6.2 absorbs the complete previously-defined v0.7.0 Village Life scope and the remaining actionable Claude visual-craft recommendations. It deepens ordinary life on the existing architecture rather than adding another game framework.

## Player outcome

A normal 15–30 minute session should feel like a small life:

- understand the village through physical destinations and a compact HUD;
- own and use a recognisable home;
- complete repeatable Courier, Gardener, Designer and Village Explorer activities;
- earn server-authoritative rewards/progress;
- shop, place furniture and visibly change the home;
- see enough authored motion and interaction feedback for the village to feel alive without shipping unfinished block characters;
- optionally notice the impossible-world layer without portals carrying the entire experience.

## Four Village Life activities

### Courier

- physical parcel pickup at Courier Depot;
- four bounded server-selected destinations: Village Shop, Town Hall, Home Store and Workshop;
- visible carried parcel;
- wrong destination performs no reward mutation;
- correct destination awards server-owned coins, account XP, Courier XP and route progress.

### Gardener

- uses the existing plant, water, grow and harvest route;
- player-facing name is Gardener while persisted profession compatibility remains `Farmer`;
- visible crop stages communicate progress;
- harvest/reward/profession XP remain server-owned.

### Designer

- legitimate new furniture placement contributes to the daily/weekly `home_design` route task;
- moves, stores, rejected placements and previews do not progress it;
- raw Designer XP is not granted on every placement, avoiding a placement XP farm;
- existing bounded home-expression progression remains authoritative.

### Village Explorer

- the existing three-landmark route becomes the player-facing Village Trail;
- visits and claim remain server-observed and idempotent;
- persisted exploration fields remain compatible;
- no client-declared completion.

## Destination depth

The release deepens the existing physical identities of:

- Town Hall;
- Village Shop;
- Home Store;
- Courier Depot;
- Workshop;
- Market / Trading Post.

Destination text may assist navigation but must not be the only reason the place is identifiable. Ordinary verbs should attach to physical objects such as parcel racks, counters, shelves, displays, workbenches, boards and furniture samples rather than generic floating kiosks.

## Visual craft inherited from Claude review

- gameplay/server foundation is ahead of presentation; improve craft before feature count;
- retain the deterministic sixteen-home village cap;
- reduce uniform/grid reading through neighbourhood identity, paths, landmarks, prop clusters, sightlines and authored arrivals within existing builder boundaries;
- treat native-part prefabs as engineering fallback/authored-native content, not automatically production hero art;
- keep semantic asset IDs separate from Roblox asset IDs and reject invented IDs;
- preserve provenance, ownership, version and approval state for production assets;
- distinct civic silhouettes must survive labels-off review;
- interactions should be diegetic and physically legible;
- one starter/hero home must support a coherent playable route;
- physical verbs need visible physical feedback where practical, not toast-only confirmation;
- polish one golden route before broadening;
- commit only real exact-candidate Studio screenshots as visual benchmarks;
- unobserved visual quality remains unfinished.

## Ambient life

v0.6.2 removes the previous primitive Part-built cats and birds. No creature is preferable to visibly unfinished creature art.

Ambient characters return only through the approved asset pipeline with provenance and exact-candidate Studio evidence. Existing authored environmental/vehicle motion may continue without introducing an NPC framework.

## Hero home

The starter/hero home acceptance route requires observed ability to:

1. identify and enter the home;
2. sit/rest;
3. use at least one kitchen, water or light interaction;
4. use one open/close or storage interaction;
5. open the honest style/wardrobe preference surface;
6. buy and place one furnishing;
7. store/move content where applicable;
8. rejoin and observe persisted placement state.

## Golden route

`spawn -> understand village -> home -> physical home interaction -> Courier pickup -> assigned destination -> reward -> Home Store -> buy -> place -> rejoin`

This route receives the strongest visual, interaction, mobile and controller scrutiny.

## Safety, authority and architecture

- schema remains 11;
- client sends intent/IDs only;
- server owns prices, rewards, XP, route completion, placement validity, privacy, inventory and trade outcomes;
- existing RemoteGuard, ProfileStore, trade journal, mutation locks and DEV/LIVE separation remain intact;
- no new framework or Wally dependency;
- no custom unfiltered chat;
- no high-value trade expansion;
- no fake Roblox IDs;
- no production publishing credentials or automatic LIVE publishing.

## Evidence

Automated/source evidence proves code/build properties only. Required Studio/device rows remain `NOT RUN` until observed on the exact candidate.

A green PR does not by itself prove hero silhouettes, labels-off comprehension, home interaction quality, parcel appearance, mobile/controller usability or performance.

## Anti-scope

Do not pull v0.8 Impossible Worlds expansion or v0.9 production deployment into this release. Do not add combat, generic pet/multiplier loops, paid power, large NPC simulation, another village/home architecture or another UI/state framework.
