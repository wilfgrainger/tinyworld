# TinyWorld v0.0.4 living-world MVP test

This guide extends the v0.01 vertical slice and the v0.0.3 living-village route. It is the acceptance contract for the living-world MVP on the current checkout. It does not replace the earlier instructions; run the existing daily reward, garden, Courier, house, portal, privacy, civic, trade, and persistence checks as regression coverage.

## What v0.0.4 must show

- The v0.0.3 world remains present: stable Ground and plot surfaces, capacity-aware plots, roads/civic dressing, and one outer woods/cliffs/sea boundary.
- The HUD identifies the v0.0.4 MVP, keeps the current player/profile information readable, and shows a truthful Tiny Bike state plus the three-item life-kit count.
- A purchased Tiny Bike exists as a visible bike model, can be mounted and dismounted, follows the owning character during the session, and changes movement speed only while mounted. Buying the bike must not silently claim that a parked bike is mounted.
- The nursery, cabins, planters, flowers, lanterns, windows, and bike showcase are visible and do not create a second coplanar collision surface or a new flicker path.
- Meadow Seed, Seashell, and Wood Token each have a readable prompt and are collected by the server. A duplicate collection on the same UTC day gives no second item; a fresh UTC day can collect again.
- A player who owns the three items can use the Home Charm prompt at their own plot. The charm consumes one of each item, persists, and adds visible home dressing. Other players cannot apply it to that plot.
- The original v0.01/v0.0.2/v0.0.3 mechanics continue to work, including saved-profile entry, daily reward idempotence, garden, Courier, home upgrade/privacy, portal, boundary route, and trade contracts.

## Rojo and single-player Studio route

1. From this checkout, start `rojo serve` and connect the Studio Rojo plugin to `localhost:34872`.
2. Stop any old Play session, sync the current source, and press **Play** in the already-published private/test place.
3. Confirm the HUD says `TINYWORLD | v0.0.4 MVP`, shows the life-kit line, and reports `Tiny Bike PARKED` or `Tiny Bike MOUNTED` truthfully. Confirm the Output pane has no red errors.
4. Walk through the village. Confirm the nursery, three cabins, bike showcase, planters, flowers, lanterns, windows, streets, and boundary dressing are readable. Move across the plot borders and grass several times; no surface flicker should appear while moving.
5. If the profile owns the Tiny Bike, use its prompt to mount it, confirm the visible bike and mounted HUD state, move through the village, then use the prompt to dismount. Confirm the parked state is visible and the speed returns to the normal value. If the profile does not own it, earn or buy it through the existing transport prompt first.
6. Visit the three life-kit pickups and collect Meadow Seed, Seashell, and Wood Token. Confirm each item increases the correct HUD count, each duplicate prompt is refused for the day, and the Output pane remains clean.
7. At the owned plot, use **Add Home Charm**. Confirm the three items are consumed, the charm appears in the HUD, and the home rebuild shows the planter, flower cluster, lantern, windows, and the appropriate home tier dressing. Confirm a non-owner cannot use the prompt.
8. Run the existing v0.01/v0.0.2/v0.0.3 regression route far enough to cover fountain, garden, Courier, house/privacy, portal, boundary, and persistence.
9. Stop and rejoin. Confirm display name/outfit, coins/XP, house tier, bike ownership, bike parked state, life-kit counts, Home Charm, portal progress, and boundary route state persist. The first session after a join must not report a mounted bike until the player mounts it.

## Two-client Studio route

Use Studio's **Server & Clients** mode with two clients and the configured server capacity.

- Confirm both clients spawn into the same generated village and can see the shared cabins, nursery, roads, boundary, and each other's character.
- Confirm each player has an independent plot and can use only their own Home Charm prompt.
- Confirm a bike is owned and followed only by its owner; the other client cannot trigger the owner's mount/dismount prompt or change the owner's state.
- Confirm both clients can see life-kit pickup and home presentation changes without duplicate grants. Then run the existing two-sided Trading Post route and confirm server-side validation still protects the exchange.
- Stop the server and clients, rejoin one client, and confirm the saved profile remains consistent.

## Publish and evidence record

Publishing is a separate gate from a successful local Rojo build and a successful Studio Play session. After the source is committed and pushed, sync the same checkout in Studio and publish the place through the normal Roblox Studio publish flow. Record the exact Git SHA, the Studio place name, the publish confirmation, the Play-session timestamp, and any remaining unverified device/family checks in `docs/progress.md`.

Do not describe the place as public-launch ready until the two-client route, publish confirmation, and family/device acceptance have been completed. No live Robux products, purchase prompts, ads, or pay-to-win mechanics are part of this v0.0.4 slice.

## Computer Use evidence note

Computer Use may use runtime-only teleportation or temporary prompt hold-duration changes when its automation layer cannot reliably move the Roblox avatar or hold a normal prompt. Those aids must never be saved to the repository, published place, or player profiles. Ordinary keyboard movement, prompt timing, mobile input, and device performance remain separate family/device acceptance checks.
