# v0.6.3 Production Art & World Craft

**Status:** active corrective release after merged v0.6.2.  
**Profile schema:** 11.  
**Gameplay scope:** frozen except where an existing interaction anchor must move with its visual object.

v0.6.3 exists because post-merge Studio screenshots of v0.6.2 proved that the ordinary village still reads as a development/test map despite substantially healthier gameplay and UI foundations.

## Player-visible outcome

The next exact Studio candidate should visibly belong to the same product family as the approved TinyWorld concept brief:

- authored village rather than plots on a green board;
- layered/characterful home and civic architecture;
- believable windows, doors, porches and rooflines;
- practical lantern fixtures rather than naked glowing spheres;
- richer planting, fences, paths, street furniture and clustered props;
- four visually different neighbourhoods;
- stronger civic-centre composition and arrival views;
- warmer, grounded ordinary-life lighting;
- compact HUD retained over a world worth looking at.

## Before baseline: v0.6.2

The 11 August 2026 Studio screenshots establish the observed before state:

- elevated view exposes large flat green spaces and repeated/grid-like plot reading;
- civic centre contains primitive/slab structures, isolated props, large empty paving and naked glowing practical lights;
- starter/hero home exterior is dominated by an oversized slab roof, flat cyan window treatment and a glowing-sphere porch light;
- player avatar presentation and compact HUD are materially improved and should be preserved.

These screenshots are evidence that the v0.6.2 visual-quality bar was not achieved. They are not committed benchmark images unless separately added to the repository with provenance, but their review outcome is recorded in the v0.6.2 acceptance history.

## Architecture pass

### Homes

- remove full-footprint flat roof slabs from the visible hero silhouette;
- use pitched/layered roof construction with modest eaves, ridge and optional tier-specific secondary mass;
- frame/recess windows and reduce cyan-panel reading;
- strengthen door/threshold/porch hierarchy;
- use foundations/plinths, chimneys and selective trim;
- replace naked Neon Ball lamps with fixture geometry and bounded warm PointLights;
- keep interaction anchors and persistence semantics unchanged.

### Town Hall

Retain the clock-tower identity but improve facade depth, entrance portico/steps, windows, roof/cornice hierarchy, planting and practical lights.

### Village Shop

Make the shopfront read through framed windows, door/awning, stock/crates/planters and a stronger roof/facade hierarchy.

### Home Store

Rebuild the separate destination as a cosy furnishing showroom with a real roof silhouette, framed windows, supported awning, exterior furniture vignette and warm practical fixtures.

### Courier Depot

Keep parcel/loading semantics but strengthen roof/entry/loading structure and clustered parcel/cart props.

### Workshop

Use garage/workbench/vehicle language, asymmetric roof massing and restrained industrial practical lighting.

### Market / Trading Post

Break the visual mass into multiple stalls/tables/crates/canopies and coherent circulation. Preserve hardened trade anchors/status state.

## Village landscape pass

Do not move gameplay-owned plots merely to make an attractive screenshot unless a tested layout change proves safe. Prefer framing/filling the existing deterministic layout:

- segmented/curved approach paths;
- planted path edges;
- hedges, low fences and garden boundaries;
- tree/rock/flower clusters;
- pocket greens and seating;
- low berms/terraces where traversal remains safe;
- stronger fountain plaza;
- neighbourhood-specific visual clusters;
- visual corridors that lead toward civic landmarks.

### Meadow Lane

Stream/bridge, cottage gardens, flower meadow, soft planting and winding approach.

### Harbour Row

Retaining walls, coastal planting, fishing/dock clutter and descending path language.

### Woodland Rise

Denser canopy clusters, rocks, narrow paths and woodland fencing.

### Orchard End

Orchard groups, vegetable beds, nursery/planter cues and terrace language.

## Ambient and lighting correction

- remove all Part-built bird/cat/butterfly actors from active village source;
- keep bounded foliage/water motion hooks where they remain visually suitable;
- ordinary practical lights use physical housings + bounded warm light;
- fantasy/portal glow remains stronger than village practicals;
- tune global lighting only after geometry/composition is improved.

## Interior pass

The hero starter home receives a focused room-craft pass without changing home gameplay:

- bedroom cluster;
- kitchen appliance/counter/table cluster;
- living focal cluster;
- bathroom zone;
- storage zone;
- garden/potting relationship;
- warm fixture lighting and visible physical feedback.
