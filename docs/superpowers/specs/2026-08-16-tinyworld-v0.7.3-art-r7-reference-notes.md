# ART R7 Reference Qualities

The user-supplied reference screenshots communicate a desired quality level, not assets or layouts to copy.

Qualities to translate into original TinyWorld design:

- cheerful, bright palette with aqua/mint/pink/peach/cream/lavender accents
- soft rounded forms rather than hard slab dominance
- strong social-plaza focal point and landmark composition
- denser intentional dressing with clear negative space for play
- readable booths, signs and routes at mobile gameplay distance
- friendly toy-world proportions
- clear visual identity for activity areas and gathering spaces
- cohesive lighting and restrained glow

Do not reproduce distinctive buildings, character designs, logos, UI, prop arrangements or layouts from the references.
