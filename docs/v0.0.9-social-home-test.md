# TinyWorld v0.0.9 social and functional-home test

v0.0.9 is a deliberately bounded vertical slice after the v0.0.8 return loop.
It addresses two promises from the original TinyWorld proposal: **every player
belongs somewhere** and **your house tells your story**. It does not claim to be
the finished home-expression release or a production multiplayer launch.

## Contract

- The village has one ephemeral, server-owned **Village Walk** party with a
  maximum of four members. A player can join, leave, or be refused when full;
  party membership is not persisted.
- Plot privacy is enforced server-side for `Open`, `Friends`, and `Private`
  homes. A visit records a shared activity only when both players are in the
  same Village Walk party and the visitor is not the homeowner.
- Trade has a bounded two-minute negotiation lease, server-side item
  preflight, confirmation invalidation when an offer changes, and no exchange
  when either side lacks the offered item. Successful trades record a shared
  activity only for party members.
- Every player starts with a concrete Cosy Bed. The Home Supply counter sells
  the next essential in a fixed, fair order: Kitchen Counter (75 coins),
  Wardrobe (125 coins), and Creative Desk (175 coins). Ownership and use count
  persist in schema version 7; each owned item creates a visible, anchored
  in-house object with its own use prompt.
- The first home slice is intentionally small. Deeper rooms, decoration
  collections, home showcases, and richer interactions remain the v0.3.0 home
  expression gate in the roadmap.

## Studio route

With the published Dev experience open and the Rojo listener connected:

1. Start Play and confirm the HUD identifies `v0.0.9 SOCIAL VILLAGE` and shows
   `Items 1/4` for a fresh normalized profile.
2. Walk to the Village Walk board. Join it, confirm the board and HUD show
   `Village Walk 1/4`, then leave and confirm the count returns to the empty
   state. A second client is needed to prove another player can join and to
   exercise the four-member cap; do not infer that from this single-client
   route.
3. Walk to **HOME SUPPLY** and buy the next three essentials. Confirm the
   coins fall by 75, 125, and 175, the HUD reaches `Items 4/4`, and a fourth
   interaction says the essentials are complete without charging more.
4. Return to the owned plot. Confirm the Cosy Bed, Kitchen Counter, Wardrobe,
   and Creative Desk are actual visible objects inside the house, not labels or
   invisible placeholders. Use at least the bed and one purchased object;
   confirm a direct message and an increased `homeUseCount`.
5. Stop Play and inspect Output. There must be no red source/runtime exception.
   Save the place only through the normal Rojo-synced Studio publish flow.

## Evidence boundary

The source gate proves deterministic contracts and assembly. A single Studio
client can prove the Home Supply purchase path, visible interior construction,
owner-only use path, and join/leave feedback. Two Studio clients are required
for party privacy, visiting another player's plot, shared activity, trade
completion, and the four-member cap. A family session remains the right gate
for comprehension and desirability, not a substitute for the source or
multiplayer checks.

This remains aligned with the north star: a safe, cosy, social world where a
young creator can build a life, express identity, and eventually earn a fair
creator-led living from durable player value. v0.0.9 adds no Robux products,
ads, pay-to-win progression, or monetised access to the home essentials.
