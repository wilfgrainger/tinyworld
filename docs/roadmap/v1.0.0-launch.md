# v1.0.0 Launch

**Status:** future launch release after v0.9.0 production-beta evidence.

v1.0.0 is a feature-freeze/polish release. Major new systems require an explicit decision to move launch rather than being smuggled into release hardening.

## Launch content promise

- one polished persistent village with up to sixteen visible resident homes;
- four distinct neighbourhoods;
- a deeply functional home with the canonical 80+ furnishing floor and 20+ meaningful interactions;
- four authored impossible worlds with distinct mechanics, secrets and home payoffs;
- at least four ordinary-life/career loops worth repeating;
- at least 30 keepsakes/discoverables;
- meaningful free character expression;
- safe visiting and bounded trading;
- mobile-first input/performance evidence;
- no pay-to-win;
- original TinyWorld art/identity rather than copied IP expression.

## Launch work

Focus on:

- bug elimination;
- onboarding comprehension;
- balancing/economy tuning;
- visual/material/animation/audio consistency;
- phone/tablet/controller quality;
- device FPS/memory/load/network behaviour;
- save/migration/recovery confidence;
- social/child-safety review;
- final icon, thumbnail, description and experience metadata;
- exact-artifact DEV approval and LIVE promotion;
- last-known-good artifact and rollback runbook.

## Promotion rule

LIVE receives the exact artifact approved in DEV. Do not rebuild source between approval and promotion.

## Rollback rule

Rollback records the previous artifact/SHA, operator, trigger and profile compatibility. Code rollback may not blindly reverse persisted migrations.

## Definition of launch success

A new player can enter on a phone, create a character, understand the village, enter a believable home, use objects, earn something through ordinary life, buy/place something meaningful, visit another player safely, optionally explore an impossible world, return with a visible home payoff, and rejoin later to find their life intact.

Every LIVE build can answer:

> What source produced this? What artifact was tested? What evidence passed? Who approved promotion? How do we roll it back?
