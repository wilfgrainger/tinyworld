# Experience pillars

## Build your life

The home is a playable expression of progress, not a menu backdrop. Players can understand rooms, storage, garden state, privacy, and upgrades through physical spaces and objects. Cosmetic expression must never confer gameplay power.

## Belong to a village

The village is small enough to learn and varied enough to remember. Civic destinations have distinct silhouettes, residential areas have named identities, and shared activities make other players feel present without requiring unrestricted social systems.

## Explore impossible worlds

Portal worlds contrast with ordinary village life through scale, material, sound, and traversal. Their missions stay bounded, readable, and connected to visible physical rewards.

## Discover secrets

Landmarks, environmental details, collections, and gentle mysteries reward attention. Discovery is communicated through the world first and friendly copy second.

## Feel safe and respected

The server owns currency, XP, inventory, ownership, privacy, trades, transport, and saves. UI does not imply success before the server confirms it. Monetisation, when eventually introduced, remains optional and fair.

## Quality test

Each feature should strengthen at least one pillar without weakening authority, readability, mobile usability, or the recognizable-object contract. Features that only add telemetry, content count, or database surface area do not meet the bar.
