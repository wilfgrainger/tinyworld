# v0.8.0 Impossible Worlds

**Status:** future depth release after v0.7.0.

v0.6.0 established four authored world foundations. v0.6.1 establishes the corrected visual baseline: ordinary village life is grounded/readable and impossible worlds are allowed the strongest scale, colour, glow and surreal contrast. v0.8.0 deepens those worlds rather than multiplying shallow portals.

## Goals

- improve unique traversal/activity mechanics in Giant Kitchen, Moonlit Meadow, Cloudpost Observatory and Clockwork Orchard;
- add authored secrets/environmental storytelling without turning discovery into UI telemetry;
- improve animation, positional sound and restrained VFX behind performance budgets;
- strengthen each world-to-home/village payoff;
- improve replayability through bounded authored variants rather than procedural noise;
- tune objective length from observed session evidence;
- preserve clear silhouettes even when fantasy lighting/scale is deliberately exaggerated.

## Inherited visual rules

- portal entrances must read as intentional fantasy landmarks without black information walls;
- magical glow is allowed but may not erase silhouette or text contrast;
- labels do not substitute for authored world identity;
- portal art remains original TinyWorld expression rather than copied reference-game/film content;
- player-facing changes include exact-candidate Studio/device visual evidence before merge-ready status.

## Anti-scope

Do not add additional launch worlds until the existing four each pass distinctness, fun, performance and home-payoff acceptance. Do not create a separate portal-only progression economy.

Do not flatten the village/portal contrast by making ordinary village content equally neon/surreal.

## Acceptance focus

- an uncoached tester can describe what makes each world mechanically and visually different;
- every world contains a memorable physical secret/reveal;
- portal completion visibly changes ordinary life;
- entrances and major mechanics remain legible without floating explanation walls;
- phone/controller traversal is practical;
- world scenes remain within agreed FPS/memory/network budgets;
- required visual evidence is attached to the exact candidate.
