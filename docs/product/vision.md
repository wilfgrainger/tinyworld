# Product vision

## North star

**Build your life. Explore impossible worlds. Discover the secrets of TinyWorld.**

TinyWorld is a warm Roblox life sandbox where a player makes a home, becomes part of a village, follows everyday interests, and crosses into surprising worlds. The ordinary life must be desirable on its own; portals deepen that life rather than replacing it.

## Player promise

A player should be able to understand the important places and objects by looking at the world, not by decoding labels or database state. Their home and progression should feel personal, social play should be safe and legible, and every reward should correspond to a visible action or object.

## Product principles

- Build depth before breadth: make one village, home, profession, and portal loop delightful before multiplying them.
- Preserve trust: saves, ownership, rewards, privacy, and trades remain server-authoritative and fail closed.
- Design for free play: progression power is not sold. Future paid content must remain fair, expressive, and optional.
- Make evidence explicit: source checks, Studio play, multi-client behavior, and published-device testing are different forms of proof.
- Keep the mystery gentle: wonder comes from discovery, scale, and place—not combat or pressure.

## Current focus

v0.5.2 resets presentation around a compact HUD, a deterministic sixteen-home village, recognizable physical affordances, and one hero starter home. It does not change the profile schema or introduce a new economy.
