# v0.7.0 Village Life

**Status:** future release after v0.6.0 acceptance.

v0.6.0 establishes the scalable village/home/content architecture. v0.7.0 should deepen ordinary life rather than replace that architecture.

## Goals

- improve route/activity variety for Courier, Gardener, Designer and Village Explorer;
- deepen the physical identity and ordinary-life verbs of Town Hall, Village Shop, Home Store, Courier Depot, Workshop and Market;
- add authored ambient routines only where device/performance evidence supports them;
- balance prices/rewards/progression from playtest/analytics evidence;
- improve social visiting without weakening privacy or child-safety boundaries;
- expand production-quality home content behind the existing 80-item definition/placement model.

## Anti-scope

Do not add a new framework, combat system, generic pet/multiplier loop, a second village architecture or high-value trading without its recovery evidence gate.

## Acceptance focus

- ordinary 15-30 minute sessions remain satisfying without portals;
- players can identify civic destinations labels-off;
- four activities are worth repeating rather than merely technically complete;
- mobile/controller and performance budgets remain green on agreed devices;
- no regression in profile v11 migration/save authority.
