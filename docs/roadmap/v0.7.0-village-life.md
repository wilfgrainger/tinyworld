# v0.7.0 Village Life

**Status:** future release after v0.6.1 Visual Rescue acceptance.

v0.6.0 established the scalable village/home/content architecture. v0.6.1 establishes the corrected player-facing visual baseline and stronger visual-evidence gate. v0.7.0 should deepen ordinary life rather than replace either architecture or visual language.

## Inherited visual baseline

v0.7.0 inherits these v0.6.1 rules:

- Brookhaven-level ordinary-world readability as a quality benchmark, not copied layout/expression;
- Toca-style tactile warmth/object clarity as a broad design quality, not copied art;
- Ready Player One-style wonder/contrast concentrated in impossible worlds;
- original TinyWorld characters, buildings, props, UI and place identity;
- no primitive character fallback;
- no large always-on-top ordinary-world information walls;
- world-first compact HUD;
- hero destinations must pass observed labels-off visual evidence.

Village Life expansion must not reopen the prototype presentation mechanisms removed by v0.6.1.

## Goals

- improve route/activity variety for Courier, Gardener, Designer and Village Explorer;
- deepen the physical identity and ordinary-life verbs of Town Hall, Village Shop, Home Store, Courier Depot, Workshop and Market;
- add authored ambient routines only where device/performance evidence supports them;
- balance prices/rewards/progression from playtest/analytics evidence;
- improve social visiting without weakening privacy or child-safety boundaries;
- expand production-quality home content behind the existing 80-item definition/placement model;
- build on the v0.6.1 golden-route quality rather than broadening at the expense of coherence.

## Anti-scope

Do not add a new framework, combat system, generic pet/multiplier loop, a second village architecture or high-value trading without its recovery evidence gate.

Do not restore floating information walls, telemetry HUD chrome, primitive avatar geometry or anonymous hero fallback merely to ship more destinations quickly.

## Acceptance focus

- ordinary 15-30 minute sessions remain satisfying without portals;
- players can identify civic destinations labels-off;
- four activities are worth repeating rather than merely technically complete;
- new player-facing surfaces include the observed visual/device evidence required by the active quality contract;
- mobile/controller and performance budgets remain green on agreed devices;
- no regression in profile v11 migration/save authority.
