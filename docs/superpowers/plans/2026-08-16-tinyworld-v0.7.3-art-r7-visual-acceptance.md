# TinyWorld v0.7.3 ART R7 Visual Acceptance Plan

This plan is controlled by Issues #17 and #22.

## Published-client views

After the exact R7 candidate is merged and Roblox DEV publication succeeds, capture the following from the real client:

1. village centre wide shot
2. fountain plaza hero shot
3. harbour / Skye area
4. Finn / fishing area
5. Pip / garden area
6. residential / house row angle
7. Mara / market angle
8. NPC close-up
9. mobile HUD in village centre
10. warm-light/evening view if available

## Pass criteria

- General map has a deliberate hero composition, readable districts and no dominant empty-green-plane feeling.
- Houses have distinct silhouettes, roof depth, window/door hierarchy and lived-in dressing.
- Activity areas visually advertise fishing, boating, gardening, trading and building before prompt text is read.
- Mara, Pip, Finn, Skye and Milo read as character-grade stylised villagers rather than stacked primitive mannequins.
- No purple/checkerboard mesh corruption or runtime EditableMesh regression.
- No grass z-fighting/flicker during ordinary camera movement.
- Prompts, doors, paths and activities remain reachable and unobstructed.
- The overall first impression is dramatically stronger than ART R6: cute, colourful, welcoming, polished, fun, memorable and premium.

Engineering green does not override a failed published-client visual verdict.
