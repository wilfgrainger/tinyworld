# Product vision

## North star

**Build your life. Explore impossible worlds. Discover the secrets of TinyWorld.**

TinyWorld is a warm persistent Roblox life sandbox where a player makes a home, belongs to a village, follows everyday interests and crosses into surprising worlds. Ordinary village life must be satisfying on its own; portals deepen that life rather than replace it.

## Audience

Primary: children/families and young Roblox players who enjoy imaginative life play.

Secondary: older players who enjoy decorating, collecting, gentle exploration and social sandbox play.

The experience must be understandable without prior simulator/RPG knowledge and must work from a phone-first input/readability baseline.

## First-session promise

Within the first ten minutes a new player should be able to:

- complete safe character setup;
- find and enter their own home;
- use at least three recognisable home objects;
- visit one village destination;
- earn something through ordinary life;
- acquire/place or otherwise make one visible persistent change;
- see a portal/mystery tease without being forced into it.

## Long-term player fantasy

> This is my little life, my home, my village, and I can step through the impossible whenever I want.

Across sessions the player builds a more expressive home, useful collection, career mastery, discovered places, portal keepsakes, village relationships, cosmetic identity and personal routines.

## v1.0 non-negotiables

- one polished persistent village;
- at most sixteen resident homes visible at once;
- four visually distinct neighbourhoods;
- a deeply functional home/placement system;
- at least 80 furnishing/home items across the eight canonical categories;
- at least 20 meaningful home interactions;
- four authored impossible worlds with unique mechanics/secrets/home payoffs;
- at least four ordinary-life/career loops;
- at least 30 keepsakes/discoverables;
- meaningful free character expression;
- safe visiting and bounded trading;
- mobile-first input/performance evidence;
- no pay-to-win;
- no anonymous finished interaction geometry;
- no essential life-sandbox feature that exists only as menu text.

## Product principles

- Build depth before multiplying worlds/villages/systems.
- Preserve trust: saves, ownership, rewards, privacy and trades remain server-authoritative and fail closed.
- Design for free play: progression power is not sold.
- Make evidence explicit: source, Studio, multi-client, device and published-place evidence are distinct.
- Keep mystery gentle: wonder comes from discovery, scale, movement and place rather than combat or pressure.
- Portals feed home/village life.

## Anti-goals

TinyWorld is not:

- an idle clicker;
- a combat-first experience;
- a portal lobby;
- a menu-driven house decorator;
- a generic multiplier/pet simulator;
- a pay-to-win economy;
- a clone of Toca Boca, Ready Player One, Disney Dreamlight Valley or another existing IP.

Inspirations may guide broad qualities such as warmth, tactile play and adventurous contrast. TinyWorld must keep original characters, props, names, locations, layouts and visual expression.

## Current focus

v0.6.0 Target-State Consolidation establishes the scalable profile/content/home/security/UI/world foundations for this promise. Its release acceptance distinguishes repository implementation from runtime/device/published evidence.
