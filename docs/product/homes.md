# Homes

## Home promise

A TinyWorld home should read as a believable small life before it reads as a progression tier. Its exterior communicates ownership and welcome; its interior provides recognizable rooms, useful objects, and visible state changes.

## v0.5.2 hero home

The starter/Cosy home receives the deepest pass:

- bedroom with bed, lamp, wardrobe, mirror, and shelf;
- kitchen with counter, sink, fridge, cooker, table, chairs, and food prop;
- living area with sofa, side table, rug, and book or toy shelf;
- bathroom with sink, toilet, and bath or shower silhouette;
- storage chest bound to the existing item chest contract;
- garden beds, planting and watering cues, potting bench, and visible growth state.

Higher tiers retain their established progression silhouette and receive the same interaction-safe foundation. v0.5.2 does not pretend to deliver a complete long-term home catalogue.

## Interaction rules

Owned furniture, upgrades, privacy, storage, gardening, and charm state remain server-authoritative. Ambient interactions may toggle a light, open a simple door or lid, or play visible feedback, but they do not mint coins, XP, inventory, ownership, or progression.

## Presentation rules

- Doors, gates, drawing boards, bells, chests, beds, appliances, and garden objects provide the affordance; prompts attach to them.
- Owner labels and room copy may clarify but cannot carry the visual identity.
- Decoration is non-collidable and bounded; explicit prompt anchors remain queryable.
- Existing profile version 10 home data remains compatible and requires no migration.
