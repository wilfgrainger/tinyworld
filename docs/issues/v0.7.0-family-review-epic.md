# v0.7.0 Family Review Epic — Exploration, Life & Mermaid Discovery

## Goal

Implement the detailed 16 Aug 2026 family/girls review as TinyWorld v0.7.0.

## Player outcomes

- Walkable hills and trustworthy terrain.
- Real swimming rather than falling into a water void.
- Near-shore swimming with Tiny Boat-gated outer-sea progression.
- A hidden whirlpool that leads to Mermaid Land.
- Five Mermaid Land quests with mermaid NPCs and server-authoritative rewards.
- Easier grass-parcel plot claiming and obvious ownership.
- A world populated by ambient and useful NPCs.
- Readable Tiny Bike, Tiny Car and Tiny Boat presentation.
- Long-term shed-to-castle home progression.
- Tiny Cat and Tiny Dog unlocks around level 10.
- Future spaceship-pad prestige without prematurely implementing full inter-world travel.

## Engineering constraints

- Reuse current Tiny Boat, plot, profile, progression and service boundaries.
- No parallel client-authoritative economy/ownership/reward state.
- Pure deterministic rules in `src/shared` and test-first.
- Roblox/world adapters in `src/server`; presentation/input intent in `src/client`.
- Keep profile compatibility unless a durable new field truly requires a migration.
- No primitive Part-built finished characters/pets/hero vehicles.
- Studio/device evidence required for visual/player-facing acceptance.

## First implementation target

Safe explorable coast only: traversal-zone rules, walkable hills, swimmable water, safe recovery, swim boundary and Tiny Boat-gated outer sea.
