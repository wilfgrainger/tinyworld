# v1.0.0 Launch

**Status:** future launch release after v0.9.0 production-beta evidence.

v1.0.0 is a feature-freeze/polish release. Major new systems require an explicit decision to move launch rather than being smuggled into release hardening.

## Launch content promise

- one polished persistent village with up to sixteen visible resident homes;
- four distinct neighbourhoods;
- a deeply functional home with the canonical 80+ furnishing floor and 20+ meaningful interactions;
- four authored impossible worlds with distinct mechanics, secrets and home payoffs;
- at least four ordinary-life/career loops worth repeating;
- at least 30 keepsakes/discoverables;
- meaningful free character expression that does not degrade the Roblox avatar baseline;
- safe visiting and bounded trading;
- mobile-first input/performance evidence;
- no pay-to-win;
- original TinyWorld art/identity rather than copied IP expression;
- ordinary-world destinations readable without floating information walls;
- compact world-first HUD rather than telemetry/dashboard chrome.

## Visual identity at launch

TinyWorld keeps the visual relationship established by v0.6.1:

- Brookhaven-level ordinary-world readability as a quality benchmark, not copied content;
- Toca-style tactile warmth/object clarity as a broad design quality, not copied art;
- Ready Player One-style wonder/contrast concentrated in impossible worlds;
- original TinyWorld characters, buildings, props, layouts, UI and names.

Hero content includes player character presentation, starter/home exteriors/interiors, civic destinations, primary vehicles and portal landmarks. Hero content must look production-intentional and pass observed exact-candidate evidence.

## Launch work

Focus on:

- bug elimination;
- onboarding comprehension;
- balancing/economy tuning;
- visual/material/animation/audio consistency;
- labels-off destination/object comprehension;
- character/avatar quality;
- phone/tablet/controller quality;
- device FPS/memory/load/network behaviour;
- save/migration/recovery confidence;
- social/child-safety review;
- final icon, thumbnail, description and experience metadata;
- exact-artifact DEV approval and LIVE promotion;
- last-known-good artifact and rollback runbook.

## Promotion rule

LIVE receives the exact artifact approved in DEV. Do not rebuild source between approval and promotion.

## Rollback rule

Rollback records the previous artifact/SHA, operator, trigger and profile compatibility. Code rollback may not blindly reverse persisted migrations.

## Definition of launch success

A new player can enter on a phone, see a coherent character, understand the village from the world itself, enter a believable home, use recognisable objects, earn something through ordinary life, buy/place something meaningful, visit another player safely, optionally explore an impossible world, return with a visible home payoff, and rejoin later to find their life intact.

The ordinary village must remain understandable without black floating system panels. The HUD must leave the world visually dominant. Portal worlds must feel more impossible than the ordinary village because contrast, not constant spectacle, carries the fantasy.

Every LIVE build can answer:

> What source produced this? What artifact was tested? What evidence passed? Who approved promotion? How do we roll it back?
