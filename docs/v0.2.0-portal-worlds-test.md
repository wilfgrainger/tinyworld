# TinyWorld v0.2.0 portal worlds test

v0.2.0 extends the existing Giant Kitchen portal with a second, reusable
destination: **Moonlit Meadow**. It is a content/framework slice, not a claim
that the full portal roadmap or a production multiplayer service is complete.

## Local verification

Run from the repository root:


## Studio route

1. Start Play from the current Rojo-synced place and confirm the HUD reads
   `TINYWORLD | v0.2.0 PORTALS`, with `Portals` and `Mission finds` copy.
2. Walk to the portal court. Confirm both `The Giant Kitchen` and `Moonlit
   Meadow` portal labels are visible and use the **Moonlit Meadow** prompt.
3. Confirm the player is moved into the separate Moonlit Meadow space. The
   sign should explain the mission, and the meadow should contain pond/trees/
   flowers plus three visible `MOONLIT SEED` objects.
4. Collect all three seeds. Confirm the HUD mission count reaches `3/3` and
   each prompt produces one direct message; the same seed cannot be collected
   twice during a run.
5. Use the return portal. Confirm the player returns to the village, receives
   the Moonlit Meadow reward, and the portal world attribute returns to
   `Village`.
6. Stop Play and inspect Output. Record any DataStore queue/session warning
   separately from red source/runtime errors. Do not use command-bar writes to
   manufacture completion or alter source.

## Runtime evidence captured 2026-08-09

- The Rojo-synced Play session showed both village portal labels and a working
  **Enter Moonlit Meadow** prompt. The arrival screen showed the separate
  meadow sign, portal, pond/trees/flowers dressing, and visible seed objects.
- Seed 1 and Seed 2 were collected through their normal proximity prompts. The
  first route exposed a presentation/accessibility defect: Seed 3 was above a
  practical player route and its prompt could not be reached. The source was
  corrected by lowering Seed 3 onto a normal ground-level pedestal, then the
  place was synced and the route was rerun.
- In the rerun, Seed 3 displayed a normal ground-level **E Collect Seed**
  prompt, the HUD reached `Mission finds 3/3`, and the return portal prompt
  sent the player back to the village. The completion toast showed `+350
  coins, +225 XP, +1 Meadow Seed`; the HUD then showed `Portals: 3
  completion(s)` and `Mission finds 0/3`.
- The stopped Studio Output contained the Rojo disconnect plus the known
  Studio DataStore queue and competing-session warnings. No red source/runtime
  exception was emitted during the corrected route. The warnings remain a
  Studio persistence-environment dependency, not evidence of published
  cross-server correctness.

## Evidence boundary

The deterministic tests and guard prove the reusable contract and concrete
assembly. A single Studio client proves the visible second-world route and one
completion. Two-client behavior, published-place parity, cross-server
persistence, scale, and family/device desirability remain separate evidence
gates.

Moonlit Meadow is intentionally a nature/storybook space: wonder and discovery
without combat, paid access, random paid rewards, or pay-to-win progression.
